03What Expromo specifies — and what's yours to decide

We tell you

  • How many PSUs / main inlets each size has
  • The connector type on the screen (C20)
  • Power draw, from the official datasheet
  • That each main inlet is a fully independent AC supply entry

You decide

  • Breaker type, curve and rating
  • RCD/RCBO selection
  • How many circuits/phases to use across the inlets
  • Cable sizing, per local regulations

04Before you start

  1. Each main inlet can be wired to its own circuit and phase, or not — that split is your call, based on the power figures above and local code.
  2. The PSUs use zero-cross relays, which limit inrush at switch-on — a normal factor to weigh in your own device selection.
  3. Mount the cabinets, wire the mains, and connect the low-voltage 12 V PSU control harness before any LED module is fitted. Power on and confirm the screen switches correctly first.
  4. Removing the C20 inlet and landing phase conductors directly on the PSU screw terminals is supported — certified electrician only.
