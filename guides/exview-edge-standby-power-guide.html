eXview Edge — Standby & Power Modes

What Active and Eco standby actually do, the two timers that trigger them, and how to wake the screen back up.

Where to find this on the screen: Apps & Settings → Settings → Device Management → Standby Mode

01Two ways to rest

This is set once, and decides what "standby" actually means every time the screen powers down.

Active Standby Factory default
14W

roughly what a couple of LED light bulbs use

The picture and sound turn off, but the screen quietly keeps its network connection alive.

Reachable over the networkcan be checked on and switched back on remotely
Tested on a real screen Putting the screen into Active Standby and immediately waking it back up again took about a minute in total, with two short reboots (~25 seconds each) along the way.
Eco Standby
≤0.5W

about 28× less power than Active Standby

Almost everything shuts off, including the network connection, to save as much energy as possible.

Not reachable over the networkcan still be woken with the power button, an IR remote, or RS232 — the yellow, upward-facing 3.5 mm jack on the sending card
Best for Screens left unused for long stretches where nobody needs to check on or wake them remotely.
Rule of thumb: keep Active Standby if the screen needs to stay reachable from control software or a control room. Only switch to Eco Standby for screens that sit unused for long periods and don't need remote wake.

02What decides when it happens

Separately from which standby mode is selected above, two timers in the same menu decide when the screen starts winding down after being left alone. They run in this order:

1

Blackout

menu: blackout time switch / time

Turns the picture fully black once the screen has been idle for a while. It's still fully powered and turns back on instantly — this isn't standby, just a blank picture.

A screensaver (showing a picture instead of black) is available as an alternative, if preferred — only one of the two can be on at a time.

10 mindefault · 1–60 min available
2

Sleep

menu: standby time switch / time

Puts the screen into full standby — Active or Eco, whichever was chosen above.

20 mindefault · 5–60 min available
